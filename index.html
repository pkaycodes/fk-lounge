01The Lounge

A little hideout.
A lot of night.

Tucked off the K Boat strip at Dome Pillar 2, FK Lounge is where Accra comes to exhale.

Timber beams, hanging gardens, warm bulbs and an open sky set the scene. The bar keeps things classic, the kitchen keeps things coming, and the pool table keeps things interesting.

Come for one cocktail. Stay for the next set.

Good people. Good music. Good drinks. Good times.

See you under the lights.

02The Experience

Four ways to stay
a little longer.

Everything happens around the same warm little corner of Dome: the terrace, the games, the stage and the bar. Pick a spot. The night will find you.

01

The Terrace

Open-air, under the bulbs. Hanging gardens, timber beams and a breeze that makes one more round feel inevitable.

02

The Games Room

Red felt, darts and a stack of games. Pull up, pick a cue, and let the night get a little competitive.

03

The Stage

Live bands, highlife and resident DJs. The room turns up a notch after nine.

04

The Bar

Cocktails with a point of view, cold bottles, and a shelf of Ghanaian authentics worth working through.
